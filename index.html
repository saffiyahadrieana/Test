Introduction

A gallery painted with purpose

Galeri Wan brings together Wan's Original Works — ten paintings that draw from the colours, textures and quiet symbolism of Malaysian heritage, from the geometry of songket thread to the tiled five-foot ways of George Town, Penang. Each piece is offered as a limited, one-of-a-kind original, available to a small circle of collectors.

Every acquisition carries meaning beyond the canvas. This collection has been created and shared in support of a cancer fundraising effort — a small act of art in service of a larger act of care.

A note on the cause — 70% of proceeds from this collection are dedicated to cancer aid, supporting treatment and care for those affected. Thank you for considering a piece, and for being part of this act of giving.

The Artist

Abdul Rashid

Abdul Rashid is a Malaysian painter based in Penang, whose work moves between classical technique and contemporary feeling — figures, landscapes and still life rendered with a reverence for colour and craft passed down through generations. His practice draws on batik motifs, the geometric tiles of George Town's heritage shophouses, and the soft, humid light of home.

Wan's Original Works marks a personal chapter: ten paintings created and shared to raise funds in support of cancer treatment and care, with 70% of proceeds dedicated to cancer aid — offered to collectors who value both artistry and intention.

— Abdul Rashid
